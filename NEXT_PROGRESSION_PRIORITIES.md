# Next update: progression priorities

Approved by Stef on 23 October 2026 for the next update, alongside the Cow Queen finale and Zak shop-only correction.

The two selected priorities:
1. Better-paced levelling. Keep the first upgrade quick; target level 4–5 after the first area instead of the current six-choice path to level 7. Space later upgrades through the journey. Level 20–22 before the Queen is a provisional playtest target, not a hard cap or validated balance.
2. Distinct build specialisations. Make upgrade choices support different useful approaches for each hero, using existing attack/special/defence systems. Ricky examples: heavy bricks, returning trowels, defensive walls. Ensure a limited upgrade budget can produce a strong focused build without making one path mandatory.

Design requirement: fun and challenging. Keep base attacks satisfying, upgrades visibly useful, and enemy attacks readable. Account for reduced automatic health/power growth when reducing levels. Do not simply reduce XP and leave progression starved, inflate boss health, or silently weaken heroes. Avoid compulsory trade-offs or a new branching UI without review; these are not required to deliver specialisation.

Validation when implemented: measure upgrade timing and menu interruptions; test focused and mixed builds, new and progressed saves, all heroes' ability to reach and fight the Queen, and difficulty with fewer picks. Human playtesting is required for fun and challenge; automated survival is not balance proof.

This records next-update scope only. No gameplay changes or publication performed.
