# Mimi — approved playable sprite direction

Approved by Stefan on 15 October 2026: **Option 3 — Little Headband**, from the simple three-option Mimi board. This is the sprite design reference, not the selection portrait.

- Petite charcoal-black Little Ninja cat, mint-green eyes, pink nose and inner ears.
- Purple forehead headband tied at the side, purple neck scarf, gold collar bell.
- Small fluffy pink ball at waist; short silver Shadow Sword with purple hilt.
- Simple rounded Canvas-style head/body and curved tail; exactly two widely spaced detached floating paws; no arms, legs or feet.
- Preserve the simple playable style shown by the user's Stef reference. Earlier detailed/anime-like concepts were rejected.
- Selection portrait should use the same identity rendered in the established warm, detailed 3D cartoon selection-art style, and was approved by Stefan on 15 October 2026.
- No Mimi gameplay implementation or release yet. Original identity recovered from archived versions: Little Ninja, petite/nimble/brave, Shadow Sword and alternative Shadow Daggers. Proposed new mechanics remain subject to design development.

## Approved gameplay — 10.2.0
Shadow Sword: longer reach, faster swings, wider arc, every third swing return slash, then Vanish. Vanish: successful hit triggers up to 1 second invisible invulnerability; next sword attack deals 300% damage and ends it; 8 second trigger cooldown, no empowered-hit retrigger. Nine Lives: 3/4/5/6/8/9 pounces, prioritising distinct targets, boss fallback repeats; final tier leaves one 4 second clone at 50% base sword damage. Shadow Step has distance, protection, slowing shadow, cooldown and next-hit double damage upgrades. Sprite option 3 and exact woodland portrait approved. User playtest pending.
