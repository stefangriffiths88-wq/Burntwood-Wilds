## Try these

1. Use a fresh/private tab to test the new opening. Start as Jackson, clear the two encounters, collect XP and rescue Ava beside the town gate. She becomes available next journey; Jackson’s active build stays intact.
2. Defeat Bruce at the rugby club. Joseph joins you, shops open, the purple curse lifts and the home landmark recovers.
3. Rescue Evie/ducks at Chasewater, Havannah at the Elder Lane Burntwood Park playground, Tom at the quarry passage and Jodie at Sankeys. Break the nearby glowing knot and reach the friend. At the quarry, free Harvey’s portal equipment and reach him too.
4. Night Owl unlocks Zak; the Bramble Beast unlocks Immy. Clear all five areas to break the Queen’s spell and recruit Mimi. This uses the existing five-area ending, with no new Queen boss encounter.
5. Recruit Stefan at his workshop for5 essence. This is separate from his10-essence signature and Jackson’s once-only gift. Cancel/purchase/reload should behave correctly.
6. Upgrade Ava’s special to Glitterstorm. Its gold/pink target should stay nearby and show where the bomb lands. Defeat a frozen ordinary enemy to see icy fragments and hear a crack.
7. Compare each cursed area and its restored state. Trees should recover; rocks, stumps, vines and bushes should vary. Home entrances show distinct danger landmarks and broken trails along roads, with nearby objectives.

Existing earned characters and upgrades are retained. This preview uses separate journey/progression save keys; it can initially read existing permanent progress but never writes production save keys. Private tabs may discard saves when closed.

Automated checks cover mechanics, saving/migration and phone layouts; native iPhone Safari, audio feel, readability and human balance remain Stef’s playtest.
